Beer Catalogue API deployed.

1. Wait for the LoadBalancer to get an external address (can take ~2 min on EKS):

   kubectl get svc {{ include "beer-catalogue.fullname" . }} -w

2. Once EXTERNAL-IP shows a hostname, your API is reachable at:

   http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/api/v1/beers
   http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/swagger-ui/index.html

3. Health (used by k8s probes):

   http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/actuator/health/readiness
   http://<EXTERNAL-IP>/actuator/health/liveness

If pods stay "0/1 Running", check readiness - usually the DB is unreachable:
   kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name=beer-catalogue --tail=50
